About the School

A Legacy of Learning, Discipline & Values

Bihar Vikas Vidyalaya is a co-educational, English-medium school committed to developing in each individual the knowledge, habits and character needed to serve both society and the nation with purpose.

Our teachers guide students through disciplined study, moral education and a supportive learning environment — so that every child grows not only in academics, but as an articulate, confident and responsible individual.

  • English-medium, co-educational instruction
  • Experienced and dedicated teaching staff
  • Regular lesson study and class tests
  • Discipline, moral education & holistic growth
Discover Our Approach

Academic Excellence

A Clear Path from Primary to Senior Secondary

A structured academic journey that grows with the student, from strong foundational learning to focused preparation for board-level examinations.

  1. 01

    Primary Education

    Class I – V

    A warm, encouraging foundation that builds literacy, numeracy and curiosity through attentive, child-focused teaching.

  2. 02

    Middle School

    Class VI – VIII

    Students widen their understanding across subjects while developing study habits, reasoning skills and confidence.

  3. 03

    Secondary Education

    Class IX – X

    Focused, exam-oriented learning based on the CCE pattern, preparing students for board-level assessments.

  4. 04

    Senior Secondary

    Class XI – XII

    Advanced study guided by subject specialists, with preparation for higher education and competitive goals.

The school's class-wise subject offerings follow the applicable board curriculum. For the current academic structure, please contact the school office.
