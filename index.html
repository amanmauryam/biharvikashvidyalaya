About Our School

A Learning Environment That Inspires Growth.

Bihar Vikas Vidyalaya is a co-educational, English-medium school in Kuchaikote, Gopalganj, offering education from Classes I to XII. As part of Bihar Vikas Educational Trust, the school is committed to academic learning alongside the all-round development of every student.

Our approach values strong fundamentals, disciplined study and a supportive environment in which children feel encouraged to ask questions, take part and grow into confident, responsible individuals. Learning is guided through continuous and comprehensive assessment so that progress is understood and supported at every stage.

Explore our academic journey

Our Philosophy

Education That Shapes More Than Academics.

Three ideas guide everyday learning at Bihar Vikas Vidyalaya.

Knowledge and Understanding

Clear concepts, strong fundamentals and a genuine understanding of subjects form the base of learning at every class level.

Character and Responsibility

Discipline, good manners, respect for others and a sense of responsibility are encouraged as part of everyday school life.

Curiosity and Personal Growth

Curiosity and participation are encouraged so that students develop confidence, interests and skills beyond the textbook.
